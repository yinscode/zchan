Topology

Every agent reaches every service through one solver.

Each agent opens one channel, to the solver. The solver keeps one channel to each service and pays it as agents buy. No agent needs a channel per seller.

Two ways in

For the agent that pays. For the service that gets paid.

Agents

Give an agent a budget, not a card.

  • One channel pays every service behind the solver, with no account or API key per seller.
  • The channel's capacity is a spending cap the agent cannot exceed.
  • Speaks x402 v2 and MPP, whichever the service offers.
$zchan-agent open --capacity 100000 --shielded
Services and merchants

Charge per request without changing your API.

  • Proxy mode answers 402 in front of any HTTP service, with a price per path.
  • One channel from the solver carries every agent's payments to you.
  • Earnings arrive at a shielded address when the channel closes.
$zchan-merchant --upstream http://localhost:3000 --routes '/api/*=500'

Protocol

Five messages. No block to wait for.

One real payment from the hosted playground, timed from the agent's first request.

01Merchant

Offer. A request without payment gets 402 and a fresh invoice the merchant signed, offered over x402 and MPP.

1 ms
02Solver

Quote. The solver checks the invoice and its liquidity, then signs a quote: amount plus fee.

5 ms
03Agent

Sign. The agent signs its channel's next total, with a payment id that makes retries safe.

15 ms
04Solver → merchant

Forward. The solver raises its own channel to the merchant, and the merchant signs a receipt.

15 ms
05Agent → merchant

Fetch. The agent repeats the request with the receipt. The merchant serves it once.

17 ms

Privacy

The public chain sees two transactions, not every purchase.

For a channel funded from shielded notes and closed cooperatively.

On chain
  • The channel's size
  • When it opened and closed
Hidden from the chain
  • Every payment and its amount
  • Which services were paid
  • Where the funds came from
  • How the channel split
Seen by the solver
  • Which service each payment went to
  • Each amount and resource path

Refunds and unilateral closes are transparent transactions and reveal the split.
