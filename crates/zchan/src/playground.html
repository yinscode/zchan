Try Zchan on Zcash testnet.

You get an agent of your own. Shield in, open one channel, pay merchants over x402 or MPP, then shield out. Payments take milliseconds; opening and closing wait for testnet blocks.

1

Shield in

The house wallet sends your agent — as an Ironwood note. Nothing about it is transparent.

2

Open a channel

Your agent funds one channel to the solver straight from that note. The solver signs your refund before the funding is signed, so the money can always come back.

3

Pay merchants

The fast part. Each payment is one signed channel state: no transaction, no block and no network fee. The solver pays the merchant on its own channel, and the merchant signs a receipt your agent presents. The solver's 100 zat service fee shows in each quote.

4

Shield out

A cooperative close: the solver builds it, and your agent signs only after decrypting its own change. Both sides are paid to Ironwood. It needs more than 10,000 zat of payments first, because a shielded close costs that much more than a plain one, paid from the solver's share.
