Shield in. Pay per request. Shield out.

This page gives you an agent of your own on a live Zcash network. Fund it privately, open one payment channel, pay three merchants over x402 and MPP, then close. The ledger on the right shows exactly what the chain saw.

Sub-second paymentsEach payment is one signed channel state, paid and answered in milliseconds. No block to wait for.
GaslessNo transaction per payment, so no network fee per payment. Only the open and the close go on chain.
PrivateFunded from Ironwood and closed to Ironwood. The chain sees a channel, not your payments.

1Shield in

The house wallet sends your agent — as an Ironwood note. Nothing about it is transparent. It waits for one block.

2Open a channel

Your agent funds one channel to the solver straight from that note. Before the funding is signed, the solver signs your refund, so the money can always come back. One block.

3Pay merchants

This is the fast part. Each payment is a signed channel state: it completes in milliseconds, with no transaction, no block and no network fee (the solver's own small service fee is shown in the quote). Pick a protocol and a merchant. The solver pays the merchant on its own channel, and the merchant signs a receipt your agent presents.

no channel yet

4Shield out

Close cooperatively: the solver builds the close, and your agent signs only after decrypting its change with its own key. Both sides are paid to Ironwood. One block. A shielded close costs 10,000 zat more than a plain one, paid from the solver's share, so it needs more than 10,000 zat of payments first.
